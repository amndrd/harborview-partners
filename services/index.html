Technology-Driven Operations & Visibility

Every service delivered by Harborview Partners is supported by advanced, integrated technology platforms that provide structure, control, and transparency across the supply chain.
CargoWise
Our international freight and customs operations are managed through CargoWise, enabling full documentation control, regulatory compliance, shipment tracking via our client portal, and financial accuracy across global movements. 
MachShip
Harborview Partners provides clients with a streamlined domestic transport portal, giving access to live rates, seamless booking, real-time tracking, and delivery updates across land transport. Through MachShip, our aggregator platform, clients benefit from our established carrier contracts and transport network.

CORE SERVICES  
Air Freight
Fast freight. No delays at altitude

Time-critical air freight services designed to meet demanding transit requirements across global trade lanes. Harborview Partners manages the full movement lifecycle—from carrier selection and routing to documentation and delivery coordination—ensuring speed, control, and consistency.

Our services include express, priority, and deferred airfreight options, along with consolidation programs and direct carrier solutions. We actively manage capacity, mitigate uplift risks, and maintain schedule integrity to support time-sensitive supply chains.

Ocean Freight
Ocean freight built for volume and reliability.

Comprehensive ocean freight management across full container load (FCL), less-than-container load (LCL), and specialised cargo movements. Harborview Partners delivers structured control over routing, carrier selection, and scheduling to optimise cost, reliability, and transit performance.

We manage carrier contracts, space allocation, and shipment planning across key trade lanes, supported by consolidation strategies and supplier coordination to improve freight efficiency and delivery certainty.

Customs Brokerage
Compliance handled. Borders cleared.

Licensed customs brokerage delivered internally, ensuring full control over compliance, accuracy, and clearance outcomes across multiple jurisdictions.

Our services include tariff classification, customs valuation, duty and tax calculation, and regulatory compliance management. We also coordinate biosecurity, IFIS and quarantine requirements, including import permits, regulatory lodgements, and directed treatments such as fumigation and inspections.

Warehousing & 3PL
Your inventory, exactly where it needs to be.

Scalable warehousing and fulfilment services designed to support inventory control, order processing, and distribution across B2B and B2C supply chains.

Our 3PL capability integrates seamlessly with international freight, customs, and transport operations, providing end-to-end visibility and operational continuity. Services include inventory management, pick and pack, cross-channel distribution, and tailored storage solutions.

Project Cargo
Complex cargo. Managed from every angle.

Harborview Partners operates a dedicated Project Cargo Division, led by specialist management with expertise in complex logistics execution.

We manage the end-to-end delivery of oversized, heavy lifts, and non-standard cargo, including route assessments, feasibility planning, permit applications, and engineered load configurations. Cargo is moved using specialised equipment such as flat racks and open-top containers, as well as breakbulk, RO/RO, multi-modal and vessel charter solutions. Our approach ensures full compliance, risk mitigation, and operational precision across all stages of project logistics ensuring safe and efficient transport for even the most challenging project shipments.

Reliable and performance-managed transport solutions across local, metropolitan, and interstate networks.

Harborview Partners coordinates both asset-based and partner carriers through a structured transport framework, ensuring consistent service levels, delivery accuracy, and full visibility across all movements. Performance is actively monitored against defined service standards to maintain accountability and reliability.

Freight Consolidation Programs 
Smarter shipments, lower cost per unit.

Strategically designed consolidation programs that combine compatible purchase orders into planned air and ocean freight shipments.

By establishing clear consolidation rules and exceptions, monitoring ex-factory timelines, and maintaining supplier visibility, Harborview Partners improves freight utilisation, reduces cost per unit, and enhances delivery predictability while supporting working capital efficiency.

Regulatory risk, managed and optimised.

Specialist advisory services focused on managing regulatory risk and optimising trade outcomes.

Harborview Partners provides support across tariff classification, duty optimisation, concession eligibility, and trade agreement utilisation. We also assist with compliance reviews, audit preparation, and ongoing regulatory strategy to ensure clients operate efficiently within evolving global trade frameworks.

Supply Chain Optimization
Advisory that turns data into performance.

Advisory-led support to analyse, design, and improve supply chain performance.

We work with clients to identify inefficiencies, optimise transport modes, restructure consolidation strategies, and improve overall supply chain resilience—delivering measurable cost, time, and operational benefits.

Quarterly Business Reviews
Transparency, built into every quarter.

Harborview Partners provides structured performance reporting and Quarterly Business Reviews (QBRs) to support transparency, accountability, and continuous improvement across our clients’ supply chains. Delivered on request, QBRs provide a detailed review of logistics activity managed through our services, including shipment volumes, transit performance, cost trends, and service reliability across all modes.

These sessions are designed to deliver actionable insights, identify opportunities for optimisation, and align operational performance with broader commercial objectives. Reviews may include KPI/SLA analysis, trend identification, risk observations, and recommendations to improve efficiency, reduce cost, and enhance supply chain resilience. By combining operational data with strategic review, Harborview Partners ensures our clients maintain full visibility and control while continuously improving supply chain outcomes.

Protection for every movement, every mile.

Harborview Partners works with an affiliated insurance partner, Midas, to provide comprehensive freight and marine cargo insurance solutions.

Through this partnership, we can arrange tailored coverage for shipments moving globally, helping protect your goods against loss, damage, or unforeseen events throughout the supply chain. Our team ensures insurance is seamlessly integrated into your logistics process, giving you greater certainty and risk management across every movement.
